📖 How to Play Paranoia & Exposed

💡 10-Second Example Round

Rahul privately reads "Who here is most likely to text their ex tonight?" and whispers it to Priya. Priya says "Kabir!" out loud to the room, then flips the 3D Coin of Fate — if it lands 🔥 HEADS (EXPOSE), everyone finds out what the question was; if it lands 🔒 TAILS (VAULT), Kabir never finds out why he was named!

🪙 Mode 1: Classic Paranoia (Whisper + 3D Coin)

1. Secret Whisper: The Asker unlocks a secret "Who in this room..." question and whispers it to the Answerer.

2. Out-Loud Name Drop: The Answerer names a person in the room OUT LOUD. No context allowed!

3. 3D Coin of Fate: Flip the coin! 🔥 HEADS exposes the secret question to the room. 🔒 TAILS locks the question in the Vault forever!

🎯 Mode 2: Most Likely To: Exposed (Room Poll)

1. Read the prompt out loud to the whole room.

2. Every player secretly votes for who fits the prompt best (Pass 1 Phone or join via Multi-Phone Room Code).

3. Reveal the Exposed Receipt Card showing the #1 voted friend and exact vote percentages!

📺 Common Screen / TV / Laptop Stage Mode

Put a Laptop, TV, or Projector in 📺 TV / Laptop Stage Mode to show the live 3D Coin of Fate, Room Code, and Vote Tracker on the big screen while everyone whispers & votes privately on their phones!
