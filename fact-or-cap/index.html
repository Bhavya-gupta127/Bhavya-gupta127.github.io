1. 📜 The Incomplete Bizarre Fact

Every round, an outrageous real-world event or science oddity appears with a missing blank (e.g., "In 2016, a man threw a live ________ into a Wendy's").

2. ✍️ Write A Convincing Lie (Cap)

Every player secretly writes a believable fake answer. If you accidentally know the real answer, the game awards you a +500 Genius Bonus and lets you write a lie!

3. 🎯 Vote: Fact or Cap?

All player lies are shuffled together with the one real fact. Everyone votes for what they believe is the real truth!

4. 🤑 Scoring The Receipts

• +500 pts for sniffing out the actual Fact.
• +1,000 pts for every friend who got tricked and voted for your Cap!
