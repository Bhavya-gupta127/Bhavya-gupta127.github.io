About PartyNights.online

Bringing people together with instant, frictionless house party games — zero app installs, zero paywalls, zero login barriers.

The Story Behind PartyNights

Have you ever hosted a house party or flatmate get-together where someone suggested playing a party game, but:

  • No one had the physical \$40 board game box.
  • Console party packs required \$30 Steam licenses and a laptop hookup.
  • Mobile alternatives demanded that all 8 friends download a 150MB app, sign up with email, and remember passwords.

By the time everyone downloaded the app, the energy in the room had vanished. We founded PartyNights.online to eliminate this friction entirely.

Zero Friction, Pure Social Fun

Every single game on PartyNights.online runs 100% in your mobile or desktop web browser. You can start playing in 5 seconds flat with two flexible modes:

📱 Pass 1 Phone (Couch Mode)

Only one person needs a phone. The app acts as the moderator, guiding secret role peeks, bluff timers, and voting rounds as friends pass the device around the room.

📺 TV Board + Phone Controllers

Cast the shared table board to a living room TV, monitor, or projector, while players scan a QR code on their own phones to privately vote, whisper, and check secret roles.

Our Game Collection

Our arcade is continually expanded with original and beloved party party genres:

  • Mr. Alien: Fast-paced social deduction and word bluffing. Civilians share a secret word while the Alien bluffs using a subtle hint.
  • Secret Dictator: Intense political intrigue and secret ballot passing inspired by classic legislative deduction games.
  • Wavelength: Telepathic mind-reading party game where players dial in clues between conceptual extremes.
  • Paranoia: Exposed: Suspenseful 3D coin flips and hilarious "Who's Most Likely To" secret accusations.
  • Charades Rush: Forehead tilt charades with 360+ cards spanning Bollywood, Hollywood, and pop culture.
  • Fact or Cap: Bizarre real trivia and bluffing where real facts sound like lies, with anti-spoiler Genius Bonus mechanics.

Our Principles & Family Mode

We believe great party entertainment should be inclusive. In addition to spicy after-hours content for adult flat parties, every game includes a 1-tap 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Mode that automatically filters out adult, alcohol, or sensitive prompts so families, kids, and campus clubs can enjoy wholesome game nights together.

How We Sustain the Project

We are committed to keeping PartyNights.online 100% free for everyone forever. To cover server and networking infrastructure, we display unobtrusive banner ads and optional rewarded breaks strictly outside active gameplay rounds via Google AdSense. We never interrupt active social clues, discussions, or card passing.
