### 1. 👽 Mr. Alien (Undercover / Impostor Deduction)
* **The Loop**: Everyone is given the same secret word (e.g. *"Pizza"*), but one hidden player is **Mr. Alien** who only receives a vague hint (e.g. *"Melted"*). Players take turns giving subtle 1-word clues in order. After discussion, vote out the suspect before the Alien deduces the secret word!
* **Features**: **740+ words** across Global & Desi editions (Bollywood, Cricket, Chai & Street Food, Campus Memes).
* **Game Modes**: Pass 1 Phone, Online Multi-Device Rooms, and 📺 Dedicated TV Radar Screen.

### 2. 🧠 Wavelength (Telepathic Spectrum Dial)
* **The Loop**: One Psychic peeks at a secret target zone on a semicircular spectrum between two conceptual extremes (e.g. `Cold ← 80% → Hot`) and gives a 1-word clue (e.g. *"☕ Fresh Chai"*). The squad debates, turns the interactive dial, and locks in their guess to score a bullseye!
* **Features**: 235+ telepathic spectrum cards across 6 decks + Family Mode.

### 3. 👑 Secret Dictator (Shadow Regime Deduction)
* **The Loop**: 5–10 players are secretly divided into Loyalists and Syndicate conspirators. Nominate Presidents and Chancellors, enact legislative policy cards, deploy executive investigations, and protect the Republic.
* **Features**: 5 immersive world themes (Shadow Regime, Space Fleet, Mobster Syndicate, Cyberpunk AI, Fantasy Kingdom).

### 4. 🪙 Paranoia: Exposed (Whisper & 3D Physics Coin Flip)
* **Mode 1 (Classic Paranoia)**: Privately whisper a spicy question to a friend, who points to someone out loud! Flip the 3D Coin of Fate—if it lands Heads, the secret question is exposed to the entire room!
* **Mode 2 (Most Likely To: Exposed)**: Secret squad voting on spicy prompts with full percentage receipts and exposed podium.

## ⚡ Game Architecture & Play Modes

### 📱 Mode 1: Pass 1 Phone (Zero Setup Couch Mode)
* Perfect for road trips, flights, picnics, and living rooms where only 1 device is in use.
* Players take turns viewing secret roles/words on a single phone with built-in privacy gates.

### 📺 Mode 2: Common TV / Laptop Board + Phone Controllers ("Jackbox-Style")
* Open `PartyNights.online` on a common **Living Room TV, Monitor, or Laptop** in `📺 TV Board Mode`.
* Friends scan the QR code or enter the 5-letter Room Code on their mobile browsers.
* The big screen acts as the public spectator board (showing dials, policy tracks, and vote counts with **100% secret isolation**), while individual player phones act as private controllers!
