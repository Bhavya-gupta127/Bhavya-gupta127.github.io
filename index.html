Frequently Asked Questions

What is PartyNights.online?
PartyNights.online is a free web arcade featuring instant multiplayer party games (Mr. Alien, Secret Dictator, Wavelength, Paranoia, Charades Rush, and Fact or Cap). Zero app downloads or accounts required.

Is PartyNights.online connected to any crypto, NFT, or metaverse platform?
No. PartyNights.online is completely independent and has zero affiliation with cryptocurrency, NFTs, or metaverse platforms (such as PartyNite). All games are light HTML5 party experiences played with friends on phones or TV screens.

Are all party games on PartyNights completely free?
Yes. Every game is 100% free with zero paywalls. We support hosting and server infrastructure through non-intrusive banner ads that never interrupt active gameplay.

Do players need to create accounts or download apps?
No. Zero account registration or downloads required. Simply open partynights.online on any smartphone, tablet, or laptop browser to begin playing instantly.

Can we play with just one phone at a house party?
Yes! Games like Mr. Alien, Charades Rush, and Paranoia feature Pass 1 Phone (Couch Mode), allowing 2 to 20 friends to play together using just a single smartphone passed around the circle.

How does TV Board Mode work with phone controllers?
Open PartyNights.online on your smart TV, projector, or laptop to display the shared table board. Players scan the on-screen QR code with their phone cameras to use their smartphones as private, secret game controllers.

Is PartyNights.online family and kid friendly?
Yes. A 1-tap Family Mode toggle is available across all games, instantly filtering out any adult, spicy, or sensitive topics to ensure 100% clean entertainment for family gatherings and kids.
