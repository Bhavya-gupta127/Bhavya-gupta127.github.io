📖 How to Play Wavelength

1
The Psychic Peeks 👁️

One player is the Psychic. They open the shutter to see where the secret bullseye sits between two extremes (e.g., Cold ↔ Hot or Overrated ↔ Underrated).

2
Give One Clue 🗣️

The Psychic closes the shutter and gives a single concept clue (e.g. "Surface of the Sun" or "Iced Chai"). No numbers, percentages, or words on the card!

3
Debate & Turn the Dial 🎯

Everyone else debates where that clue belongs on the 180° spectrum and drags the glowing needle to lock in their guess.

4
Tap to Reveal & Score ⚡

Open the shutter! Score 4 pts for the green Bullseye, 3 pts for the gold inner zone, or 2 pts for the orange outer zone. In Team mode, the opposing team can also steal +1 pt by guessing Left or Right!
