📜 Official Rules & Executive Powers

🛡️ Republic Victory (Blue Team)

Enact 5 Blue Policies OR Execute the Secret Dictator using a late-game Executive Power.

🐍 Syndicate Victory (Red Team)

Enact 6 Red Policies OR Elect the Secret Dictator as Chancellor any time after 3 Red Policies have been enacted!

🏛️ Each Round: 3 Steps

  1. Nominate & Vote: The Presidential Candidate nominates an eligible Chancellor. Everyone votes AYE or NAY simultaneously. If rejected 3 times in a row, Chaos auto-enacts the top card of the deck!
  2. Legislative Session: The President secretly draws 3 Policies, discards 1 face-down, and passes 2 to the Chancellor. The Chancellor secretly enacts 1 and discards 1. (Both can lie about what they drew!)
  3. Executive Action: If a Red Policy is enacted onto a power slot, the President must immediately use that slot's power:
    • 👁️ Policy Peek (5–6P): Secretly view the top 3 cards of the draw deck.
    • 🔍 Investigate Loyalty (7–10P): Secretly inspect one player's Party Membership (Blue or Red).
    • ⚡ Special Election (7–10P): Pick any player to be the next President.
    • 💀 Execution (All Boards, Red 4 & 5): Permanently eliminate a player. If they are the Secret Dictator, Blue wins immediately!
    • 🚫 Veto Power (Unlocked at 5 Red): Chancellor and President may mutually agree to discard both drawn policies.
